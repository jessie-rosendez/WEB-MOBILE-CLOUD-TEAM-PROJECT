## Common Technology Terms

**HyperSpeed Wireless** — Phantm's proprietary 2.4GHz wireless protocol. Delivers sub-1ms latency, equivalent to a wired connection. Used across mice, keyboards, headsets, and controllers.

**8000 Hz Polling Rate** — Device reports its state 8,000 times per second. That's 8× the standard 1000Hz polling rate used by most competitors. Results in measurably faster input response in competitive games.

**Chroma RGB** — Phantm's addressable per-zone lighting system. Supports 16.8 million colors per zone. Available on mice, keyboards, mouse mats, and speakers. Syncs across devices via the Phantm app.

**THX Spatial Audio** — Licensed spatial surround processing from THX Ltd. Available on headsets (BlackShark V3 Pro, Kraken V4 Pro) and speakers (Nommo V2 Pro). Creates a three-dimensional soundscape from stereo or surround sources.

**Focus Pro 35K Sensor** — Phantm's flagship optical sensor. Rated at 35,000 DPI. Zero smoothing, filtering, or acceleration — raw input only. Used in Viper V4 Pro and DeathAdder V4 Pro.

**Optical Switches** — Actuation triggered by a light beam rather than physical contact. Eliminates debounce delay. Rated 70–100M activations. Used in mice, keyboards, and controllers.

**HyperFlux** — Phantm's wireless charging technology for gaming peripherals. Powers compatible mice while in motion across the full mat surface. Used in HyperFlux V2 charging mat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are the prices on the site the real retail prices?**  
A: No. All prices shown are placeholder estimates for class project planning purposes. They are not live retail prices.

**Q: Is the Phantm Spectre X available now?**  
A: The Spectre X is in Kickstarter preview phase. Early backer price is $799. Sign up at phantm.com to be notified when the campaign launches.

**Q: What is the return policy?**  
A: 30-day return window for unopened or lightly used items in original packaging. Email support@phantm.com with your order number to start a return.

**Q: What warranty do Phantm products come with?**  
A: All electronics and premium accessories include a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturer defects.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**  
A: No. Phantm ships within the United States only during the current launch phase.

**Q: What wireless technology do Phantm products use?**  
A: Phantm uses HyperSpeed Wireless — a proprietary 2.4GHz protocol with sub-1ms latency. It's equivalent to a wired connection for gaming purposes.

**Q: Can I use a Phantm keyboard on both PC and Mac?**  
A: Yes. Phantm keyboards with Tri-Mode Connectivity support USB-C wired, 2.4GHz wireless, and Bluetooth, making them compatible with PC, Mac, and mobile devices.

**Q: What is the difference between the Blade 14, 16, and 18?**  
A: All three are gaming laptops with the same GPU tier (NVIDIA RTX 40-series) and 8000Hz polling rate. The difference is screen size and form factor: 14" ultra-portable ($2,399), 16" slim immersive ($2,999), 18" desktop replacement ($3,499).

**Q: What makes the Spectre X different from other gaming chairs?**  
A: The Spectre X is Phantm's original design. It adds features not found on standard gaming chairs: reactive RGB base lighting, a hidden gear storage tray under the seat, and a modular side-rail mount system for headsets and controllers — all in a racing shell silhouette built for visual identity.

**Q: How do I contact support for chair assembly help?**  
A: Email support@phantm.com or call (919) 555-0142 Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM ET.

**Q: What is the Phantm Thunderbolt 5 Dock used for?**  
A: The TB5 Dock expands a single Thunderbolt 5 laptop port into a full workstation hub: dual 8K display outputs, 4× USB-A, 2× USB-C, 2.5G Ethernet, and 96W laptop pass-through charging. Designed for creators, streamers, and power users.

**Q: Does the HyperFlux V2 charge any phone?**  
A: The HyperFlux V2 supports simultaneous Qi wireless phone charging alongside the proprietary mouse charging surface, so any Qi-compatible phone will charge.
