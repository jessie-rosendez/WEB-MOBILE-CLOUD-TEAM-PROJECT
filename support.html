Frequently Asked Questions

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also log in to your Phantm account and view live shipment status under Order History. Note: tracking may take up to 24 hours to activate after dispatch.

Phantm accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for unused products in original packaging. Opened products may be subject to a restocking fee. To start a return, contact support with your order number. The Phantm Spectre X chair requires freight return shipping — see the warranty page for details.

The Phantm Spectre X is currently in Early Backer pre-order. Early Backer units are estimated to ship in Q3 2026. Placing an order now locks in the Early Backer price. Full production pricing will be higher at general release.

All Phantm hardware is covered by a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware failures, and dead-on-arrival units. It does not cover physical damage, water damage, or unauthorized modifications. To file a claim, contact support with your order number and a description of the issue.

Most Phantm peripherals (mice, keyboards, headsets) work on Mac and Linux as plug-and-play HID devices. Advanced features like per-key RGB programming and polling rate adjustment require the Phantm Configuration software, which is currently Windows-only. Mac support is on our roadmap.

Phantm Configuration software and all firmware updates are available in the Downloads section of your Phantm account. You can also find product-specific drivers by searching your product name on the Support page. Always update firmware before contacting support for performance issues.
